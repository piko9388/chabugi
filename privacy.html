Privacy Policy

App: CarTally: EV Cost Tracker (iOS) · Effective date: 2026-11-19

CarTally does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The developer of CarTally (“the developer”) receives no data through the app.

  • There is no account and no sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The developer runs no server. The app does not send or receive data over the network.
  • There are no ads, no analytics tools, and no tracking. The app includes no third-party data collection SDKs.
  • The app does not use your location.

2. Data stored on your device

The following information, which you enter yourself, is stored only on your device. The developer cannot see it.

  • Vehicle details: nickname, model, model year, battery capacity, odometer readings, and similar details
  • Charging records: date and time, charger name, unit price, energy charged, amount, odometer reading, notes
  • Charger and payment settings: charger names and unit prices; payment method names, card issuer, discount rate and cap. The app never asks for a card number.

3. Reading a payment screenshot

When you choose a photo of a payment screen, the app reads the text on your device and fills in the entry fields. All of this happens on the device. The photo and the text read from it never leave your device.

4. Backup files

  • Once a day, the app saves a copy of your records (a CSV file) on your device. The copies are in the Files app under “On My iPhone › CarTally”, and only the 30 most recent are kept.
  • When you tap Export, the app hands a file in the same format to the destination you choose. Where that file is kept and who it is shared with is up to you.
  • Backup files exist only on your device and in the Files app. They are not sent to the developer.
  • If you have turned on device backup for your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer), the app’s data may be included in that backup. That backup is managed under your Apple Account and Apple’s policies, and the developer has no access to it.

5. Sharing with third parties

None. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share with or hand over to anyone else.

6. Deleting your data

  • You can delete records in the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes the records on the device together with the copies in the “On My iPhone › CarTally” folder.
  • Files you exported elsewhere remain where you put them until you delete them yourself.

7. Children’s privacy

CarTally does not collect personal data from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Statistics provided by Apple

If you have chosen in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with statistics and crash reports that do not identify you. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy; the app itself does not collect it.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s features change in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
